About Me

The through-line is useful software with real-world consequences

I like building at the point where software, product judgment, and domain expertise have to meet. Code is the tool I use to turn ideas into usable systems, but the work only matters when it fits the people and decisions it is meant to support.

Right now, that work is Mantic. I'm co-founding an AI-powered wildfire management startup focused on bringing regional risk maps, simulations, and decision support closer to wildfire management and emergency response workflows.

Before Mantic, I worked as a software developer at Ericsson, where I led a team of 10 developers on the company's biggest project to date. That experience shaped how I think about engineering quality, team execution, and building systems that can survive contact with real operational needs.

I have a Computer Science Master's degree from the University of Amsterdam and Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, graduating Cum Laude. My thesis on energy issues in microservice systems won the award for best Master's thesis in the Netherlands for 2023 from VERSEN, the Dutch National Association for Software Engineering.

My background in Finance and Accounting keeps me close to the business side of product decisions. I care about software that is technically sound, understandable to stakeholders, and worth building.

What I'm Building

Mantic - Wildfire Management Operational Center

Mantic

Know where to focus

Mantic is a wildfire management operational center. The product helps teams prioritise attention with regional risk maps that rank areas by vegetation, terrain, and what is at risk, so monitoring and prevention can focus on hotspots instead of low-value areas.

My work sits between product, engineering, and applied AI: shaping workflows around complex models, building reliable interfaces, and translating technical output into something decision-makers can use.

I care about the full path from idea to deployment: understanding the problem, making the product clear, keeping the software practical, and learning quickly from the people who would rely on it.
