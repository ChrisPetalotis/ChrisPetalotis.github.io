What I'm Building

Mantic - Scaling AI-powered wildfire management

Mantic

Bridging science and practice in wildfire management

I'm co-founding Mantic, a startup dedicated to improving wildfire management and community resilience. The problem is clear: climate change is making wildfires more complex and devastating every year. 2023 was the worst fire season in Europe in two decades, and a single California fire in 2025 caused nearly $30 billion in damages. Yet decision-makers still rely on outdated methods, and the latest wildfire research—while valuable—often doesn't fit real-world operations.

At Mantic, we're changing that. Our mission is to bridge the gap between science and practice in the global disaster management landscape. We take the latest validated research and transform it into operational tools for governments and businesses. Our platform lets users simulate wildfire scenarios, test preventive actions, and visualize risk with simple clicks. Using GenAI, we translate scientific simulation results into actionable insights and use-case-specific suggestions.

Right now, I'm focused on scaling Mantic to reach more professionals and communities. Every day, I'm working to grow the platform so that emergency responders, government agencies, and businesses can access cutting-edge research to assess risk, train their teams, and optimize their planning for future events. This is what I'm dedicating my time and energy to—building something that matters and scaling it to make the biggest impact possible.
